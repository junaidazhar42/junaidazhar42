## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🔷 [Uber Fare Prediction — End-to-End ML App](https://github.com/junaidazhar42/Uber-Fare-Prediction)
> *Machine Learning · Streamlit · HuggingFace Spaces · Geospatial*

Built a full end-to-end ML pipeline trained on **200,000+ NYC Uber trips** to predict ride fares in real time. Users enter any NYC address or landmark, geocoded live via the **OpenStreetMap Nominatim API**, and get an instant fare estimate with a **Folium route map**. Benchmarked Linear Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost — the tuned **Random Forest** model (via GridSearchCV) took the crown and was serialised with joblib. Deployed publicly on **Hugging Face Spaces**.

`Python` `Scikit-learn` `XGBoost` `Streamlit` `Folium` `Pandas` `NumPy`

### 🔷 [Walmart Weekly Sales Forecasting — Time Series Analysis](https://github.com/junaidazhar42/Walmart-Weekly-Sales-Forecasting-ARIMA-Prophet)
> *Time Series · SARIMA · Facebook Prophet · Forecasting*

A rigorous time series forecasting study across **45 Walmart stores (2010–2012)**. Uncovered key business insights — **Store 20** led with $301.4M in total sales and **holiday weeks drove ~7.84% higher sales** on average. Built and evaluated 5 models: AR, MA, ARIMA, SARIMA, and Facebook Prophet. The breakthrough came from correcting SARIMA's seasonal period from `s=13` → `s=52` to align with annual holiday cycles, achieving a best **MAPE of 1.73%** — proving that precise seasonal configuration outweighs model complexity.

`Python` `Statsmodels` `Facebook Prophet` `SARIMA` `Matplotlib` `Seaborn`

### 🔷 [YelpSphere — Geo-Based Business Clustering](https://github.com/junaidazhar42/YelpSphere-Geo-Based-Cluster-Analysis)
> *Unsupervised Learning · Geospatial · DBSCAN · HDBSCAN*

Applied three clustering algorithms — **K-Means, DBSCAN, and HDBSCAN** — on a dataset of **150,000+ US Yelp businesses**. Used Haversine metric on radian coordinates for true earth-surface distance in DBSCAN, producing **17 geographically meaningful clusters with only 0.63% noise**. K-Means on lat/lon coordinates achieved a silhouette score of **0.9678**. Each cluster was profiled by star rating, review count, open rate, dominant cities, and top business categories.

`Python` `K-Means` `DBSCAN` `HDBSCAN` `Folium` `Plotly` `Scikit-learn`

> *NLP · FastAPI · Multi-class Classification*

End-to-end sentiment analysis system on **~8,200 customer reviews** (positive / neutral / negative).
